How the flow works

Step 1

Your spec

Each instrument is set up against its own customer’s card. This batch carried two specs — Sweetwater and Standard — applied per instrument, never by model alone.

Step 2

Bench setup

Two passes: preparation first (nut channels, pegs, tailpiece, strings), then bridges and string height, measured as the work is finished.

Step 3

Triple check

Final measurements, a blind re-check against the card, and a pre-pack check of bridge, fittings and serial before anything is boxed.

Step 4

Signed record

A signed certificate with the actual values of every instrument, its serial number, and any condition notes — delivered with the batch.

Batch notes

1. Bridge blanks, as received. Several bridges in this batch arrived from the factory with feet thinner than the nominal dimension — recorded exactly as measured (4.8 mm on units SW-10, SW-11 and SW-13) — and some blanks showed coarse factory sanding marks running across the grain; these were dressed and minimized during setup. None of the accessory bags in this batch included a spare bridge.

2. Layout templates. String spacing at the nut and at the bridge was laid out using Strobel templates (16.5 mm / 34 mm); the values recorded above are the actual measurements of each finished instrument.

3. Measurement conditions. All measurements were taken in the workshop after final adjustment. Wood and strings respond to changes in temperature and humidity, so string heights may vary slightly under different ambient conditions or after transport.
