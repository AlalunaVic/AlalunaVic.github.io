How do I know my bow needs a rehair?+

It skates on the strings even with fresh rosin, lots of hairs have broken (often on one side), the ribbon looks thin, or the hair has stretched so the screw turns a long way and it still feels loose. If you play most days, plan on every 6 to 12 months.

How long does it really take?+

Drop it off in the morning and it’s ready that afternoon. Ask about our express service: about two hours, for a small extra charge.

How do I make my rehair last?+

Loosen the hair every time you put the bow away, keep your fingers off the hair, rosin lightly, keep the case away from heat and humidity swings, and open it often: tiny “bow bugs” feed on hair in closed cases.

Mongolian, Siberian or black hair?+

Premium Mongolian white hair is included: even, strong and smooth. Siberian is thicker with more bite, an upgrade many cellists and bassists like. Black hair is the coarsest and grippiest, traditional for bass, and quoted at drop-off.

Do you work with music shops and schools?+

Yes. Shops, schools and youth orchestras can set up regular pickups with volume pricing. Shop bows can come back in two or three days when there’s no rush.

Do you rehair carbon fiber bows?+

Yes, carbon and wood bows alike, for violin, viola, cello and bass.
