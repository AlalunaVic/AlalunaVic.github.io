Handing over your bow is hard. Here’s what we hear most.

Your bow is part of how you play. Before you let anyone touch it, these are fair questions to ask, and these are our answers.

I can’t be without my bow for two weeks.

You won’t be. It comes back the same day.

Same day is our standard service, not a paid upgrade. Drop it off in the morning, pick it up in the afternoon, play your own bow that evening.

Concert in a few hours? Express takes about two hours, for $20 more.

What if something happens to my bow?

We check it with you before any tool touches it.

At drop-off we look over the tip, the stick, the frog and the eyelet together, and note anything already there. You leave knowing exactly what condition your bow is in.

Victor has rehaired thousands of bows over 25 years, from student sticks to fine French pernambuco.

Will the new hair be any good?

Premium Mongolian stallion hair, chosen and combed by hand.

We set the hair length to your bow so the ribbon lies flat and even, with the right tension at the frog. Siberian hair is available as an upgrade; just ask.

What if it doesn’t feel like my bow anymore?

You play it before you leave.

At pickup you try the bow right there. If the tension, the balance or the feel is off, we adjust it on the spot. Still not right? We redo it, free.

I’ve never heard of this shop.

How do I know my bow needs a rehair?+

Common signs: the bow won’t grip the string even with fresh rosin, many hairs have broken on one side, the ribbon looks thin or uneven, or you can’t get the tension right. Most players who play daily rehair every 6 to 12 months.

How long does it really take?+

Drop it off in the morning and it’s ready that afternoon. Express service takes about two hours, for $20 more.

What if I can’t pick it up the same day?+

Your bow stays safe at the workshop and you pick it up when it suits you, at no extra charge.

Where are you?+

The workshop is in Woodbridge, NJ, by appointment. We also pick up bows across New Jersey and New York City, and you can ship your bow to us.

Do you work with music shops and schools?+

Yes. Shops, schools and youth orchestras can set up regular pickups with volume pricing. Shop bows can come back in two or three days if you don’t need them the same day.

What if the rehair doesn’t feel right?+

Try it at pickup and we adjust it on the spot. If it still isn’t right, we redo it, free.
